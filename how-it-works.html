How Sandbox.io Works

Discover our transparent, 100% asset-backed, and Shariah-compliant Murabaha investment model.

The 4-Step Investment Process

We connect forward-thinking investors directly with verified rural agricultural, fisheries, and livestock projects in Bangladesh.

Step 01

Browse & Select

Explore verified agricultural and livestock campaigns on our active marketplace. Review expected returns, tenures, and specific project locations across Bangladesh.

Step 02

Murabaha Financing

Instead of lending cash with interest (Riba), funds are utilized to procure actual physical commodities and agricultural inputs under structured Murabaha agreements.

Step 03

Monitoring & Growth

Our team and field partners closely supervise the operational cycle—from crop growth and trading to livestock nurturing—ensuring safety and asset integrity.

Step 04

Profit Distribution

Upon successful harvest, sale, and cycle completion, principal capital plus the halal profit margins are distributed back to your account.

Our Commitment to Shariah Principles

Strictly No Interest (Riba): Traditional lending involves interest-bearing loans, which are strictly prohibited. Sandbox.io operates strictly on trade-backed, partnership, and cost-plus-markup (Murabaha) principles where returns are generated from real economic value creation.

100% Asset-Backed Security: Every investment corresponds directly to physical goods (such as agricultural commodities, livestock, or machinery), eliminating speculative exposure and ensuring complete transparency for all participants.
